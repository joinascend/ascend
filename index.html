Three stages. One outcome.

A fixed sequence with no shortcuts — every member moves through the same standard, in the same order.

Four weeks of live and recorded training covering discovery, objection handling, closing frameworks and CRM fluency — taught by coaches who close six and seven figures a year themselves.
