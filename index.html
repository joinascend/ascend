The Opportunity

Remote. High-income. In demand for life.

High-ticket sales means selling premium offers — $2,000 to $20,000+ — for other companies, on commission. Pre-qualified people who already want what's being sold, structured conversations, and a real decision at the end of the call.

High price point, high commission. Fully remote, every time — a skill you build once and carry into any industry, for life.

100% Remote Commission-Based Setter or Closer Path No Degree Required

How The Model Works

Setter Qualifies leads and books calls for a Closer. Often the entry point into the industry.
Closer Runs the sales call and closes the deal. Higher commission, higher skill ceiling.
Pay Commission per sale, uncapped. No salary ceiling on your output.
Location Anywhere with a laptop and wifi. No office, no commute, no relocation.
